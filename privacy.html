Privacy policy

This app ("n8n") is a private tool used only by its owner to sort their own Gmail inbox on their own home server.

It accesses only the owner's Gmail account. No data is shared with, sold to, or sent to any third party. Email content is processed locally and is not stored outside the owner's own Google account and home server.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections.
